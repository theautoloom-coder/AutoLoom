AUTOLOOM Design direction · mobile + web · light

Complex data underneath.
A counter tool on top.

The daily loop is khata: maal gaya, udhaar likha, shaam ko paisa aaya. Every screen here is built so the number the owner opened the app for is the largest thing on it, and so a bill can be finished in under twenty seconds at a busy counter. Red stays the only brand colour — it marks what you can act on, and nothing else.

Surface
Hairline card — lists, panels
Keyline card — the one thing that matters
Red spine — active, actionable, mine
No blurred drop shadows. Depth is a hard 3px offset, like a printed slip on a glass counter — it survives cheap Android screens and direct sunlight.
Motion
Entrance, once. 380ms cubic-out, 55ms stagger, capped at 8 rows. A list assembles instead of snapping.
Count-up on takings. 700ms, only the figure the owner opened the app for.
Press: scale 0.97, 120ms. Sheets spring in from the bottom, 260ms.
Nothing ambient. This screen is looked at fifty times a day; anything decorative becomes noise by the third look.
Your existing useEntrance and useCountUp already do exactly this — kept as-is.
